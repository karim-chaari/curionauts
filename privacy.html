Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

"Curionauts TV pipeline" is the private publishing tool of Perigee TV (formerly Curionauts TV). I am its only user. It signs in to my own YouTube channel, my own TikTok account, and my own Perigee TV Facebook Page and Instagram account to publish the videos I make, and does nothing with anyone else's account.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does with YouTube: uploads my videos (always as private), sets their title, description, thumbnail, captions and playlist, and manages my channel's own private live broadcasts. It reads back only my own videos to check they were saved correctly.

Facebook and Instagram (Meta)

The tool uses a Meta app ("perigeeTV") with Facebook Login for Business, only to publish the channel's own videos and captions to the Perigee TV Facebook Page and the @perigee.tv Instagram account, which I own. It reads back only those posts to check they were published. It does not read, collect or store data about people who view, like, comment on or message the Page or the account.

What data it keeps

  • The sign-in tokens for my own accounts. They are kept in files on my own machines that only my user can read, never shared, sold or sent anywhere else.
  • The metadata of my own videos that the tool itself writes (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions).

It does not collect, read or store any data about viewers or about other people's channels. It sets no cookies and has no analytics.

Deleting the data, revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (third-party access). When access is revoked, or on request, the stored tokens are deleted within 7 days; there is no other stored API data to delete. TikTok access can be removed in the TikTok app under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions. Facebook and Instagram access can be removed in Facebook under Settings, Business integrations; see data deletion.

Google API data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
