Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 (TikTok added)

"Curionauts TV pipeline" is a private tool used only by the channel's owner. It signs in to the owner's own YouTube account with Google OAuth to upload the channel's own videos (always as private), set their thumbnails and captions, and manage the channel's own private live broadcasts. It also signs in to the owner's own TikTok account (Login Kit and Content Posting API) to send the channel's own videos there as private posts or drafts.

What data it uses

  • The OAuth tokens for the owner's YouTube and TikTok accounts and the TikTok account's basic profile (name, avatar), stored only on the owner's own machines, never shared or sold.
  • The channel's own video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions) that the tool itself creates.

It does not collect, store or share data about any other person, and it does not read viewers' data.

Google API data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
