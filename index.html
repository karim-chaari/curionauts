Curionauts TV

Curionauts TV is a science channel for curious people of all ages. Professor Nova and Professor Mike present the day's science news and explain how we know what we know, with drawn documentaries and short formats in between.

Every fact on the channel comes from a named source, and every picture is licensed and credited.

The publishing tool

I make the channel on my own computers. A small tool of mine, "Curionauts TV pipeline", uploads the finished videos to my own YouTube channels using the YouTube API Services, and to my own TikTok account. Nobody else signs in to it. Uploads are private; I make a video public myself, in YouTube Studio.
