Perigee TV

Perigee TV (formerly Curionauts TV) is a science channel for curious people of all ages. Professor Nova and Professor Mike present the day's science news and explain how we know what we know, with drawn documentaries and short formats in between.

Every fact on the channel comes from a named source, and every picture is licensed and credited.

The publishing tool

I make the channel on my own computers. A small tool of mine, registered as "Curionauts TV pipeline", uploads the finished videos to my own YouTube channel using the YouTube API Services, to my own TikTok account, and to my own Perigee TV Facebook Page and Instagram account. Nobody else signs in to it. YouTube and TikTok uploads are private, and I make a video public myself; the Facebook Page and Instagram posts are public.
