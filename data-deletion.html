Data deletion

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Perigee TV's publishing tool signs in only to the channel's own accounts. It stores no data about anyone else: no viewer, follower or commenter data from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook or Instagram.

If you signed in to the "perigeeTV" Meta app (only the channel's owner does), you can remove it at any time: in Facebook, go to Settings, then Business integrations, and remove "perigeeTV". Removing it revokes its access, and the stored sign-in token is deleted within 7 days.

To ask for any data to be deleted, write to Karim Chaari, chaari.karim.plus@gmail.com. Requests are answered within 7 days.
